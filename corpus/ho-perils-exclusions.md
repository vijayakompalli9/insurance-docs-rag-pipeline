# Perils Insured Against and Exclusions

## Perils Insured Against - Dwelling

Coverage A and Coverage B are written on an open perils basis: we insure against direct physical loss to property unless the loss is excluded. Coverage C is written on a named perils basis and covers fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism, theft, falling objects, weight of ice and snow, and sudden accidental discharge of water or steam.

## Exclusion - Flood

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, storm surge, or overflow of any body of water, whether or not driven by wind. Water that backs up through sewers or drains is also excluded unless the Water Backup endorsement NM-END-14 is attached. Flood protection must be purchased separately, for example through a public flood program.

## Exclusion - Earth Movement

We do not cover earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole or other earth sinking, rising or shifting. If earth movement results in a fire or explosion, we do cover the resulting fire or explosion damage.

## Exclusion - Wear, Neglect and Maintenance

We do not cover wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, rust, or damage from continuous or repeated seepage of water over a period of 14 days or more. Loss caused by your failure to use reasonable means to protect property after a loss is also excluded.

## Exclusion - Mold and Fungi

Mold, fungus and wet rot are excluded unless they result from a covered sudden water discharge that you could not detect. In that case coverage for mold remediation is limited to 10,000 dollars per policy term.
