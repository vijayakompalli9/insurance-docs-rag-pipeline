# Section I - Conditions

## Duties After Loss

After a loss you must give prompt notice to us or your agent, notify the police in case of theft, protect the property from further damage and keep records of repair expenses. You must prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing quantity, description and actual cash value. Within 60 days of our request you must send a signed, sworn proof of loss.

## Deductible

Unless otherwise noted, we pay only the part of the loss that exceeds the deductible shown on the declarations. A separate windstorm or hail deductible, expressed as a percentage of the Coverage A limit, applies where shown on the declarations.

## Loss Settlement

Covered losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost if, at the time of loss, the Coverage A limit is at least 80 percent of the full replacement cost of the dwelling. If the limit is below 80 percent, we pay the larger of the actual cash value or a proportion of the replacement cost. Until repair or replacement is complete, we pay no more than actual cash value. Personal property is settled at actual cash value unless the Personal Property Replacement Cost endorsement is attached.

## Appraisal

If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal. Each party selects a competent and impartial appraiser within 20 days. The two appraisers select an umpire. An agreement signed by any two of the three sets the amount of loss.

## Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment of premium by giving 10 days notice. When the policy has been in effect for 60 days or more, or on renewal, we may cancel only for non-payment, material misrepresentation, or a substantial change in risk, with 30 days notice.
