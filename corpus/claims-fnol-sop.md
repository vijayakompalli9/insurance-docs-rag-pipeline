# Claims SOP - First Notice of Loss (FNOL)

## Purpose

This procedure tells claims analysts how to record a first notice of loss for homeowners claims and set the claim up for the assigned adjuster.

## Intake Steps

Verify the policy was in force on the date of loss and the loss location matches the insured location. Record the date, time, cause and a short description of the loss. Ask whether anyone was injured and whether emergency services attended. Capture a callback number and a preferred contact window.

## Contact Standards

The assigned adjuster must make first contact with the insured within 24 hours of FNOL. An inspection must be scheduled within three business days, or within 24 hours when the dwelling is uninhabitable. Every contact attempt is logged in the claim diary.

## Initial Reserves

Set the initial indemnity reserve using the reserve worksheet for the cause of loss. Reserves must be reviewed at 30 days and whenever new information changes the expected outcome by more than 20 percent. Reserves above 100,000 dollars require supervisor approval.

Sample intake note used in training. Insured: Marisol Trevanian, phone (555) 214-7781, email marisol.trevanian@example.com. Reported kitchen fire on 2026-03-14. Contact person: Mr. Odell Brandvik, alternate phone 555-309-4412. SSN on file: 912-48-3307. Adjuster assigned within 2 hours; first contact completed same day.
