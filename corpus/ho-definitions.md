# Homeowners Policy Definitions

Northwind Mutual Insurance (fictional). Form NM-HO-100. This section defines words used throughout the policy. Defined terms apply whether singular or plural.

## You and Your

"You" and "your" mean the named insured shown on the declarations page and a spouse or domestic partner who lives in the same household. A person who moves out of the household remains covered until the earlier of the policy renewal or 90 days after moving out.

## Insured Location

"Insured location" means the residence premises shown on the declarations page, the grounds and structures on it, and any vacant land you own other than farm land. A newly acquired residence is an insured location for 30 days after you take ownership, provided you tell us within that period.

## Residence Premises

"Residence premises" means the one-family or two-family dwelling where you live, including other structures and grounds at that address. A dwelling with three or more units is not a residence premises under this form.

## Actual Cash Value

"Actual cash value" means the cost to replace damaged property with new property of like kind and quality, minus depreciation for age, condition and obsolescence. Depreciation may be applied to materials and labor.

## Replacement Cost

"Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation, at the time of loss.

## Occurrence

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to substantially the same harmful conditions, that results in bodily injury or property damage during the policy period.
