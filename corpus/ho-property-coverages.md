# Section I - Property Coverages

## Coverage A - Dwelling

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises, including structures attached to the dwelling such as an attached garage or deck. Materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises used to build, alter or repair the dwelling are also covered. Land, including the land on which the dwelling sits, is never covered.

## Coverage B - Other Structures

We cover other structures on the residence premises that are set apart from the dwelling by clear space, such as a detached garage, fence or garden shed. The Coverage B limit is 10 percent of the Coverage A limit unless a higher limit is shown on the declarations. We do not cover other structures rented to anyone who is not a tenant of the dwelling or used for business purposes.

## Coverage C - Personal Property

We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. Property usually located at a secondary residence is limited to 10 percent of the Coverage C limit.

### Special Limits of Liability

The following special limits apply per loss: 200 dollars for cash and coins; 1,500 dollars for securities and passports; 1,500 dollars for watercraft including trailers; 2,500 dollars for theft of jewelry, watches and furs; 2,500 dollars for theft of firearms; and 2,500 dollars for business property on the residence premises.

## Coverage D - Loss of Use

If a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we cover additional living expense: any necessary increase in living costs so your household can maintain its normal standard of living. Payment is for the shortest time required to repair or replace the damage or, if you permanently relocate, the shortest time for your household to settle elsewhere. Coverage D is limited to 20 percent of the Coverage A limit.
