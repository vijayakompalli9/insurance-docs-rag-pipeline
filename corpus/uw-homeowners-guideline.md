# Homeowners Underwriting Guideline

Internal guideline for Northwind Mutual Insurance (fictional) underwriters. Applies to new business and renewals of form NM-HO-100.

## Eligibility

Eligible risks are owner-occupied one-family or two-family dwellings used primarily as a residence. Ineligible risks include dwellings under construction, dwellings vacant for more than 60 consecutive days, properties with more than two acres used for commercial farming, and dwellings with an active foreclosure notice.

## Roof Age and Condition

Roofs with asphalt shingles older than 20 years are ineligible for new business unless an inspection confirms at least five years of remaining life. Roofs older than 15 years must be written with actual cash value roof settlement endorsement NM-END-22. Wood shake roofs are ineligible in wildfire zones 3 and 4.

## Insurance to Value

Coverage A must equal at least 100 percent of the replacement cost estimate produced by the approved valuation tool. Underwriters may accept 90 percent with documented justification. Policies below 80 percent are not accepted because they trigger coinsurance penalties at loss.

## Prior Loss History

Applicants with three or more paid property losses in the past five years are ineligible. Two water losses in the past three years require a plumbing inspection and a 2,500 dollar minimum deductible. Liability losses involving dog bites require referral.

## Coastal and Wildfire Zones

Risks within 1,000 feet of tidal water require a minimum 2 percent windstorm deductible. Risks in wildfire zone 4 require a defensible space inspection showing a 30 foot cleared perimeter before binding.

## Protection Class

Dwellings in public protection class 9 or 10, or more than five road miles from a responding fire station, require referral and a minimum 1,000 dollar all-peril deductible.
