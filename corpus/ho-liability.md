# Section II - Liability Coverages

## Coverage E - Personal Liability

If a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence, we will pay up to our limit the damages for which the insured is legally liable and provide a defense at our expense by counsel of our choice. Our duty to defend ends when the limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

## Coverage F - Medical Payments to Others

We pay necessary medical expenses incurred within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury to others. Coverage F applies regardless of fault, up to 5,000 dollars per person. It does not apply to you or regular residents of your household other than residence employees.

## Section II Exclusions

Coverage E and Coverage F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage that is expected or intended by an insured, arising out of business pursuits or rental of premises, arising out of the use of motor vehicles away from the insured location, or caused by a dog previously declared dangerous under local ordinance.

## Damage to Property of Others

We pay replacement cost up to 1,000 dollars per occurrence for property damage to property of others caused by an insured, even when the insured is not legally liable.
