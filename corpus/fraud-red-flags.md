# Property Claims Fraud Red-Flag Checklist

## How to Use This Checklist

Red flags are indicators, not proof of fraud. When three or more red flags are present, or any single high-severity flag, refer the claim to the Special Investigations Unit (SIU) within five business days. Do not accuse the insured or share the referral with them.

## Policy Red Flags

The loss occurs within 60 days of policy inception or a coverage increase. The insured recently increased Coverage C limits or added scheduled jewelry shortly before a theft. The applicant could not provide prior insurance history.

## Claim Red Flags

The insured is unusually pushy for a quick settlement or offers to accept a lower amount for immediate payment. Receipts are missing, handwritten, or all from the same vendor. Damaged items described are inconsistent with the lifestyle or the home. High-severity flags: a fire with multiple points of origin, or contents removed from the home before a fire.

## Financial Red Flags

The insured has recent bankruptcy, foreclosure, or significant overdue taxes. The property is listed for sale with no offers. The claimed loss closely matches an outstanding debt.
