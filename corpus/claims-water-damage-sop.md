# Claims SOP - Water Damage Claims

## Coverage Determination

First establish whether the water was a sudden and accidental discharge, which is covered, or flood, surface water, sewer backup or long-term seepage, which are excluded unless an endorsement applies. Ask when the leak was first noticed and look for staining, rot or corrosion that indicates seepage of 14 days or more.

## Emergency Mitigation

Advise the insured to stop the source of water and begin drying within 48 hours. Approved mitigation vendors may be dispatched without prior approval for invoices up to 5,000 dollars. Mitigation costs above 5,000 dollars need adjuster sign-off before work continues.

## Moisture Documentation

Mitigation vendors must provide daily moisture readings, photographs and a drying log. Drying is complete when readings return to the dry standard for the material. Claims without moisture documentation are not eligible for payment of drying equipment charges.

## Mold Handling

When mold is found, confirm whether it resulted from a covered sudden discharge. Covered mold remediation is limited to 10,000 dollars per policy term. Refer to an industrial hygienist when visible mold exceeds 10 square feet.
