# Underwriting Authority and Referral Matrix

## Authority Levels

Associate underwriters may bind Coverage A limits up to 750,000 dollars. Senior underwriters may bind up to 1,500,000 dollars. Coverage A limits above 1,500,000 dollars require approval from the regional underwriting manager. Any single risk above 3,000,000 dollars total insured value must be referred to the home office property committee.

## Mandatory Referral Triggers

Referral to a senior underwriter is mandatory for: a prior liability loss involving a dog bite; a dwelling in protection class 9 or 10; a home-based business with client visits; a trampoline or swimming pool without a locked fence; and any request to waive an inspection.

## Documenting Exceptions

Every exception to guideline must be documented in the policy file with the guideline section, the reason, the compensating factors, and the approver name. Exceptions without documentation are treated as authority violations during quarterly audit.

## Turnaround Standards

Referrals must be answered within two business days. Referrals pending more than five business days are escalated automatically to the regional underwriting manager.
